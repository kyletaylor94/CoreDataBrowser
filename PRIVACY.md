# Privacy Policy for CoreDataBrowser

Last updated: August 31, 2026

## Overview
CoreDataBrowser ("the App") is a developer tool designed to inspect, search, and manage local Core Data, SwiftData, and UserDefaults containers within booted iOS Simulators.

Your privacy is paramount. CoreDataBrowser is built with a privacy-first approach: **we do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or application data.**

## Data Collection and Storage
- **No Data Collection:** The App does not collect any personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or unique device identifiers.
- **Local Processing Only:** All inspection, searching, and editing operations are performed strictly on your local machine within your local Simulator containers. No data ever leaves your computer.
- **Third-Party Services:** The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks.

## Network Access
CoreDataBrowser operates entirely offline and does not establish network connections to external servers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted in this repository with an updated date.
